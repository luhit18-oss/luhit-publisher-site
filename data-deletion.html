Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: September 3, 2026

These instructions apply to information associated with LUHIT Publisher connections to supported platforms, including Meta products such as Facebook and Instagram.

How to request deletion

To request deletion of data associated with your use of LUHIT Publisher, send an email to luhitjavierlunagarcia@gmail.com with the subject LUHIT Publisher Data Deletion Request.

Please include enough information to identify the connected Facebook Page, Instagram professional account or other authorized account involved in the request. Do not send passwords or access tokens.

What will be deleted

Where applicable, LUHIT Publisher will remove locally maintained account connection information, authorization data, configuration records and other application data associated with the identified connection that is under LUHIT Publisher's control.

Data stored by Meta

Content, account information or records stored directly by Facebook, Instagram or another third-party platform are controlled by that platform and are subject to its own deletion tools, retention policies and legal obligations. Revoking LUHIT Publisher does not by itself delete content already published to those platforms.

Revoke Meta access

You may also revoke LUHIT Publisher's access through the Facebook or Meta account settings where business integrations and connected applications are managed. Revoking access prevents future API requests using that authorization.

Local application data

Because LUHIT Publisher is primarily a local desktop application, users may also remove local application data from the computer on which the application is installed. If assistance is required, use the contact address above.

Confirmation

Deletion requests will be reviewed and, when the requester and relevant connection can be identified, completion will be confirmed by reply to the requesting email.
