Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

LUHIT Publisher is a desktop publishing tool used by authorized account managers to connect and publish content to supported social media platforms, including Meta products such as Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts.

1. Information we process

When an authorized user connects a supported social media account, LUHIT Publisher may process information made available through the platform's official APIs, including account identifiers, Page or professional-account identifiers, account names, access tokens or authorization credentials, permissions granted by the user, publishing status, and information required to perform requested publishing actions.

LUHIT Publisher also processes media files, captions, publishing settings and destination selections that the authorized user chooses inside the application.

2. Meta / Facebook / Instagram authorization

Facebook and Instagram information is accessed only after an authorized user grants access through Meta's official authentication and authorization systems. LUHIT Publisher does not request or store Facebook or Instagram passwords.

The application uses authorized access only to perform functions requested by the user, such as identifying Pages or professional Instagram accounts the user is permitted to manage, publishing selected content, and retrieving limited publication information required by the application's publishing and calendar features.

3. Purpose of processing

Information is processed only to provide LUHIT Publisher's requested functionality, including account connection, destination selection, publication of user-selected content, publication status, and related local application features.

4. Local storage

LUHIT Publisher is primarily a local desktop application. Authorization information, account configuration, publication history and related application data may be stored on the user's own device so the application can maintain authorized connections and operate its features.

5. Sharing and sale of data

LUHIT Publisher does not sell personal information. Information is transmitted to Meta or another supported social media platform only when necessary to authenticate an authorized connection, retrieve permitted account information, or complete an action requested by the user.

6. Data retention

Local application data is retained only as needed for the application's operation or until the user disconnects the account, removes the local data, or requests deletion where applicable. Platform access tokens may cease to work when they expire or when access is revoked by the user or the platform.

7. Revoking access

Users may revoke LUHIT Publisher's access at any time through the applicable platform's account, business-integration or security settings. Revoking platform access prevents LUHIT Publisher from making future authorized API requests with that authorization.

8. Data deletion

Users may request deletion of data associated with LUHIT Publisher. Instructions are available on our Data Deletion Instructions page. Users may also remove locally stored application data from the device where LUHIT Publisher is installed and revoke the application's access through Meta.

9. Security

LUHIT Publisher is designed to use official platform authorization flows and APIs. Users should protect access to the Windows account and computer where the application is installed because locally stored application information may be accessible to someone with access to that device.

10. Third-party platforms

Meta, Facebook, Instagram and other supported social networks are independent third-party services. Their own privacy policies, terms and technical rules also apply to information processed by those services.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when LUHIT Publisher's functionality, supported platforms or legal requirements change. The latest version will remain available at this URL.

12. Contact
