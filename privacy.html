Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

1. Information processed

LUHIT Publisher may process basic authorized account information, authorization credentials, media selected for publishing, captions and publishing settings required to provide its functionality.

2. TikTok authorization

TikTok information is accessed only after the account owner explicitly authorizes LUHIT Publisher through TikTok's official authentication system.

3. Purpose

Authorized information is used to provide requested functionality, including account connection and publishing user-selected content.

4. Passwords

LUHIT Publisher does not request or store TikTok passwords. TikTok authentication is performed by TikTok.

5. Local data

Authorization information required by the desktop application may be stored on the user's device to maintain the authorized connection.

6. Sharing

LUHIT Publisher does not sell personal information. Information necessary to complete a requested publishing action may be transmitted to the corresponding social media platform.

7. Revoking access

Users may revoke authorization through the applicable social media platform and may remove locally stored connection information.

8. Contact

Official information about the service is available on the LUHIT Publisher website.
