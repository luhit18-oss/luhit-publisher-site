LUHIT Publisher

LUHIT Publisher is a desktop social media publishing application that allows authorized users to connect social media accounts and publish content from one workspace.

TikTok accounts are connected only after the account owner explicitly authorizes LUHIT Publisher through TikTok's official authentication process.

TikTok integration

LUHIT Publisher uses authorized TikTok API access to connect accounts, retrieve permitted account information and publish content explicitly requested by the authorized user.

LUHIT Publisher does not request TikTok passwords and does not claim ownership of content published by its users.
